## Added Custom Pokéballs!
### Mewtwo Ball
- This ball is from the first Pokémon movie!
- Ignores the current HP of the target for the catch rate calculation
- Meaning it behaves as if the Pokémon had one hp - regardless of its actual hp _(which translates to roughly a 2x catch rate at one hp and 6x at 100% hp)_
### Typing Ball
- This ball is from Pokémon: Typing Adventure
- It has a 5x catch rate boost on unown and 1x against every other Pokémon
- Was moreso added as a gimmick to test out what I can do with custom Pokéballs, might add more in the future!
